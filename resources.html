Only trust the domain, not the search ad

Scam websites copy official designs and buy ads at the top of Google. Type the address yourself or use the links on this page. A real government exam site ends in .gov.in or .nic.in, never in a random shop-like domain.

Learn for free

Free learning platforms you can start today

You do not need expensive coaching to learn the basics. These are free or free-to-start and cover school subjects, coding, design and more.

DIKSHA

Government platform with school lessons, practice questions and teacher resources mapped to your syllabus — in many Indian languages.

diksha.gov.in →

SWAYAM

Free online courses from school to university level, taught by Indian institutes. Good for building a subject early.

swayam.gov.in →

NCERT e-books

Download the official textbooks free. Class 11–12 NCERT books are the base for JEE, NEET and many other exams.

ncert.nic.in →

NPTEL

Free engineering and science video courses from IITs and IISc. Great once you are in a technical degree.

nptel.ac.in →

SWAYAM / NPTEL practice

Use the video lectures, assignments and practice tests to revise topics you find hard — at your own speed.

swayam.gov.in →

HBCSE Olympiads

The official Indian science Olympiad programme. Look at past papers even if you do not formally participate — they sharpen thinking.

olympiads.hbcse.tifr.res.in →

Skill India Digital

Government platform for short skill courses, certifications and job links across many trades and sectors.

skillindiadigital.gov.in →

National Career Service

A government job-and-career portal: career advice, skill courses and a job board for freshers.

ncs.gov.in →

You are not alone

Helplines & support

Exam and career stress is real, and it is okay to ask for help. These services are free and confidential.

♥

Tele-MANAS — 14416

India's free national mental-health helpline, available in many languages. Call 14416 (or 1-800-891-4416) if you feel anxious, low or overwhelmed. telemanas.mohfw.gov.in

♥

Childline — 1098

A 24×7 free helpline for children and young people in any kind of distress or danger. Call 1098 from anywhere in India. childhelplineindia.org

i

Talk to a trusted adult

A teacher, counsellor, parent, elder sibling or relative can help more than any website. School counsellors and career cells exist for exactly this.

i

National Career Service

Free career guidance and job information from the government, including help for students unsure about their next step. ncs.gov.in

Stay safe

How to spot an admission or exam scam

Every year students lose money and even a year of study to fraudsters. These red flags protect you.

✗

"Guaranteed admission" or "guaranteed rank"

No coaching centre or agent can guarantee a seat or a rank in a government exam. Anyone who promises this is lying.

✗

Payment to a personal account or UPI

Real exam fees are paid on the official portal, never to a personal phone number or random UPI ID. Keep every receipt.

✗

Pressure and deadlines

"Only 2 seats left, pay in the next hour" is a classic trick. Take your time and verify on the official site.

✗

Copycat websites & apps

Check the web address letter by letter. Official Indian exam sites use .gov.in or .nic.in. Bookmark the real one once and always use it.

!

If you think you have been cheated

Tell a parent or teacher immediately, save all messages/screenshots, and report it. In India you can file a cyber complaint at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930. Do not feel ashamed — it happens to smart people too.
