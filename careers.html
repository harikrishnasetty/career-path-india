Sector 01

Technology & IT careers

India's largest private-sector employer. Skill matters more than the name of your degree — portfolios, projects and internships decide your first job.

Software Developer / Engineer

Builds websites, apps and software products
High demand
PCM, Commerce, Arts (via BCA)₹4–12 LPA
PathClass 12 (Maths/CS helps) → B.Tech CSE / BCA / BSc CS → internships + projects → job
ExamsJEE Main, CUET, state CETs, university tests
SkillsProgramming (Python/Java/JS), data structures, Git, problem solving

Data Scientist / Analyst

Turns raw data into business decisions
High demand
Any stream with Maths/Stats₹5–12 LPA
PathClass 12 with Maths → B.Sc Stats/Maths, B.Tech, BCA or B.Com → projects + certifications → job
ExamsCUET, JEE, private university entrances
SkillsPython, SQL, statistics, Excel, Power BI/Tableau

AI / Machine Learning Engineer

Builds systems that learn from data
Fast growing
PCM₹6–15 LPA
PathPCM → B.Tech CSE/AI/Data Science → MSc or specialised certifications → ML roles
ExamsJEE Main/Advanced, state CETs
SkillsPython, maths-heavy statistics, ML frameworks, maths olympiad style thinking

Cybersecurity Analyst

Protects systems, networks and data from attacks
Growing
Certifications matter₹4–10 LPA
PathClass 12 (any) → B.Tech/BCA/BSc + certifications (CompTIA Security+, CEH) → SOC analyst roles
ExamsCUET, university entrances; then vendor certification exams
SkillsNetworking, Linux, ethical hacking labs, attention to detail

Cloud / DevOps Engineer

Runs and automates servers and cloud systems
Active
PCM, Commerce₹5–12 LPA
PathClass 12 → B.Tech/BCA/BSc → AWS/Azure/GCP certification + internships
ExamsUniversity entrances; cloud certification exams
SkillsLinux, networking, scripting, CI/CD, containers

UX / UI Designer

Designs how apps and websites feel to use
Active
Design at a tech company₹4–10 LPA
PathAny stream → B.Des (UCEED/NID) or BCA/BS + design certifications → portfolio → job
ExamsUCEED, NID DAT, university design tests
SkillsFigma, user research, visual design, empathy, storytelling
Sector 02

Engineering & infrastructure careers

Build the country — roads, bridges, machines, circuits, buildings and now smart infrastructure. Diploma offers a faster, practical entry into the same field.

Civil Engineer

Designs and builds infrastructure — roads, bridges, buildings
Core field
PCM / Diploma₹3.5–8 LPA
PathPCM → B.Tech Civil (JEE/state CET) or Diploma Civil → junior engineer job → lateral-entry B.Tech
ExamsJEE Main, state CETs, JEECUP/POLYCET for the diploma route
SkillsAutoCAD, surveying, project planning, site management

Mechanical Engineer

Designs machines, engines and manufacturing systems
Core field
PCM / Diploma₹3.5–8 LPA
PathPCM → B.Tech Mechanical or Diploma Mechanical → manufacturing/maintenance jobs
ExamsJEE Main, state CETs, diploma entrances
SkillsCAD (SolidWorks/AutoCAD), thermodynamics, machining, teamwork

Electrical / Electronics Engineer

Power systems, circuits, chips and electronics devices
Core field
PCM / Diploma₹3.5–9 LPA
PathPCM → B.Tech EEE/ECE or Diploma Electrical/Electronics → jobs in power, PSUs, electronics manufacturing
ExamsJEE, state CETs, diploma entrances; later SSC JE / PSU exams
SkillsCircuit analysis, MATLAB, PLC basics, soldering & lab work

Architect

Designs buildings that are safe, functional and beautiful
Creative + technical
PCM with Maths₹3.5–8 LPA
PathClass 12 with Maths → NATA / JEE Paper 2 → 5-year B.Arch → internship → licence to practise
ExamsNATA (Council of Architecture), JEE Main Paper 2
SkillsDrawing, 3D software, spatial thinking, building codes

Junior Engineer (Government)

Technical officer roles in state departments, PSUs and railways
Diploma's best route
Diploma / ITI + experience₹3–7 LPA + govt benefits
PathDiploma (3 yrs) → SSC JE / state JE / PSU recruitment → junior engineer post; can upskill to B.Tech later
ExamsSSC JE, state PSC/board JE exams, PSU recruitments
SkillsTechnical drafting, site supervision, govt exam reasoning & GK

Aerospace / Aviation Engineer

Aircraft, drones, satellites and propulsion systems
Niche
PCM₹4–10 LPA
PathPCM → B.Tech Aerospace/Aeronautical (IITs/NITs/IIST) → M.Tech or ISRO/DRDO/HAL/private aerospace
ExamsJEE Advanced (for IITs), JEE Main, state CETs
SkillsPhysics & fluid mechanics, CAD/CFD, precision, safety mindset
Sector 03

Healthcare & medicine careers

Long study, high respect, strong job security. Most routes need PCB in Class 12 and the NEET exam — but nursing, pharmacy, physiotherapy and allied health are excellent, less crowded options in the same sector.

Doctor (MBBS)

Diagnoses and treats patients; can specialise later
Prestigious
PCB₹6–12 LPA (higher after PG)
PathPCB → NEET UG → MBBS (5.5 yrs incl. internship) → practice / MD/MS via NEET PG
ExamsNEET UG (then NEET PG)
SkillsBiology mastery, patience, empathy, long-haul discipline

Dentist (BDS)

Oral health specialist with independent practice options
Active
PCB₹4–8 LPA
PathPCB → NEET UG → BDS (5 yrs) → own clinic, hospital or MDS specialisation
ExamsNEET UG, then NEET MDS
SkillsFine motor skills, biology, patient handling, business sense for a clinic

Nurse (B.Sc Nursing)

Core of hospital care; huge global demand
In-demand globally
PCB₹3–6 LPA (abroad much higher)
PathPCB → B.Sc Nursing (state/nursing entrance, 4 yrs) → hospital jobs; PG (M.Sc Nursing) or overseas options later
ExamsState nursing entrance tests / NEET-based admissions (varies by state)
SkillsClinical care, communication, composure under pressure

Pharmacist

Medicines expert — hospital, industry or own shop
Active
PCB / PCM₹2.5–6 LPA
PathClass 12 (PCB/PCM) → D.Pharm (2 yrs) or B.Pharm (4 yrs) → retail/hospital/industry; M.Pharm or Pharm.D for research & clinical roles
ExamsState pharmacy entrance tests (some states use NEET)
SkillsChemistry, accuracy, regulatory knowledge, inventory sense

Physiotherapist (BPT)

Rehabilitates patients after injury, surgery or disability
Growing
PCB₹3–6 LPA
PathPCB → BPT (4.5 yrs) → hospitals, sports teams, home care or own clinic; MPT to specialise
ExamsState/college entrance tests for BPT
SkillsAnatomy, hands-on technique, motivation skills, fitness

Veterinarian (BVSc & AH)

Animal doctor — pets, livestock, wildlife
Active
PCB₹4–8 LPA
PathPCB → BVSc & AH (5.5 yrs) via state veterinary entrances or NEET-linked quotas → clinics, govt vet service, dairy/poultry industry
ExamsState veterinary entrance exams (check current mode each year)
SkillsBiology, courage with animals, field work, farm economics

Psychologist / Counsellor

Mental health, schools, organisations and clinics
Rising demand
Arts/Psychology or PCB₹3–8 LPA
PathClass 12 with Psychology (Arts) → BA/BSc Psychology → MA/M.Sc → clinical practice, school counselling, HR/wellness roles
ExamsCUET, university entrances (UG & PG)
SkillsListening, empathy, research methods, confidentiality

Allied Health Technician

Lab, radiology, OT, dialysis and imaging technicians
Steady jobs
PCB₹2.5–6 LPA
PathPCB → BSc/diploma in Medical Lab Technology, Radiology, OT Technology, Dialysis (state paramedical entrances) → hospital/lab jobs
ExamsState paramedical admission processes / university entrances
SkillsPrecision, machine handling, infection control, empathy
Sector 04

Finance, banking & business careers

Commerce's strongest ground. Professional qualifications like CA/CS/CMA can start right after Class 12 and often beat engineering salaries by your late twenties.

Chartered Accountant (CA)

Audit, tax and financial advisory — one of India's most respected qualifications
Top earning
Any stream after 12₹8–15 LPA (on qualifying)
PathClass 12 → CA Foundation → CA Intermediate → 2-yr articleship → CA Final → membership (ICAI)
ExamsICAI Foundation / Intermediate / Final (own exam system, not a university degree)
SkillsAccounts, taxation, audit, consistency over 4–5 years of exams

Company Secretary (CS)

Corporate law, governance and compliance expert
Active
Commerce / Arts₹5–10 LPA
PathClass 12 → CSEET → CS Executive → CS Professional + training → ICSI membership
ExamsICSI CSEET and executive/professional levels
SkillsCompany law, drafting, compliance, attention to detail

Cost & Management Accountant (CMA)

Costing and management accounting for industry
Active
Commerce₹5–10 LPA
PathClass 12 → CMA Foundation → Intermediate → Final + training → ICMAI membership
ExamsICMAI Foundation / Inter / Final
SkillsCosting, budgeting, analytics, manufacturing economics

Investment Banker / Financial Analyst

Markets, valuations, mergers and equity research
High paying
Commerce + Maths best₹6–15 LPA
PathClass 12 with Maths → B.Com/BBA/BA Economics → CFA/FRM or MBA → analyst roles
ExamsCUET, IPMAT, university entrances; later CAT/GMAT and CFA
SkillsExcel modelling, valuation, economics, extreme reading stamina

Actuary

Insurance and risk mathematics — small field, very high pay
Elite niche
Maths essential₹6–15 LPA+
PathClass 12 with Maths → ACET entrance → B.Sc Actuarial Science / any maths degree + professional exams → fellow actuary
ExamsACET (Institute of Actuaries of India), then a long professional exam series
SkillsStatistics, probability, financial maths, persistence over years

Banker (IBPS / SBI / RBI)

Public-sector banking careers — officer and clerk levels
Stable govt-type job
Any graduate₹4–12 LPA
PathAny graduation (B.Com/BBA/BA/BSc) → IBPS PO/Clerk, SBI PO, or RBI Grade B exam → posting
ExamsIBPS, SBI, RBI recruitment exams (prelims + mains + interview)
SkillsQuantitative aptitude, reasoning, English, banking awareness

Digital Marketer

Runs ads, SEO, social and content for brands
Skill-based
Any stream₹3–8 LPA
PathAny degree (or even during college) + certifications + real projects/freelancing → agency or in-house roles
ExamsNo entrance exam — certifications and portfolio matter
SkillsSEO, Google/Meta ads, analytics, copywriting, creativity

Entrepreneur / Startup Founder

Build your own business — no degree required, but skills are
High risk, high reward
Any streamUnlimited (and zero)
PathAny stream → learn a skill + solve a real problem → start small (local service, reselling, content, software) → grow with customers
ExamsNone — but BBA/B.Com/engineering helps with networks and fundamentals
SkillsSelling, money management, resilience, learning from failure
Sector 05

Government, defence & public service careers

Stability, respect and pensions. Some routes (Agniveer, SSC GD) open right after Class 10, while UPSC and officer roles come after graduation.

Civil Servant (IAS / IPS / IFS)

Top administrative, police and foreign service posts
Most aspirational
Any graduate₹8–15 LPA + benefits
PathAny graduation (BA recommended for optional subjects) → UPSC Civil Services Exam (Prelims → Mains → Interview) → training
ExamsUPSC CSE — start serious reading in Class 12/first year, not later
SkillsCurrent affairs, essay writing, reasoning, GK, consistency over 2–4 years

Defence Officer (NDA route)

Commissioned officer in Army, Navy or Air Force
Prestige + free degree
Army: any stream; AF/Navy: PCM₹8–12 LPA + benefits
PathClass 12 (appearing OK) → UPSC NDA exam (April/September) → SSB interview → 3–4 yrs academy training with a degree → commission
ExamsNDA & NA (UPSC); age roughly 16.5–19.5, unmarried
SkillsFitness, maths & GK, leadership, discipline, English

Agniveer (Soldier / Sailor / Airman)

Four-year armed forces entry — some trades need only Class 10
Active
Class 10 or 12 by trade₹4.5–6.5 LPA package
PathClass 10/12 → Agniveer notification & rally/written test → physical + medical → training → 4 years service with Seva Nidhi payout at exit
ExamsAgniveer recruitment by Army/Navy/Air Force (trade-wise eligibility — verify carefully)
SkillsFitness, discipline, trade skill, team work

Police / Paramilitary

Constable to officer ranks in state police and CAPFs
Active
10th (SSC GD) / 12th / graduate₹3.5–6 LPA
PathClass 10 (SSC GD) or Class 12/graduation for state police → written exam → physical test → medical → training
ExamsSSC GD, state police constable/SI exams, CAPF exams
SkillsPhysical fitness, GK, reasoning, integrity, courage

Teacher / Professor

Shapes the next generation — school to university levels
Stable
Any stream (graduate)₹3.5–8 LPA
PathGraduation + B.Ed (school) → CTET/state TET → govt/private teacher; or PG + UGC NET for college teaching
ExamsCTET / state TET, UGC NET / SET, university faculty recruitment
SkillsSubject mastery, explaining clearly, patience, classroom management

Government Clerical / Railways

SSC CHSL, RRB NTPC and other central/state office posts
Active
12th / graduate₹3–6 LPA
PathClass 12 (CHSL) or graduation (CGL/NTPC) → written exams → typing/skill tests where required → posting
ExamsSSC CHSL, SSC CGL (graduates), RRB NTPC, state equivalents
SkillsQuant, reasoning, English, typing speed, accuracy
Sector 06

Law & legal careers

Any stream can become a lawyer — what matters is your reading, reasoning and CLAT preparation. Corporate law is among the highest-paying non-engineering careers for a fresh graduate.

Advocate / Lawyer

Represents clients in courts; litigation or corporate practice
Any stream can apply
12th any stream → CLAT₹4–12+ LPA
PathClass 12 (any stream) → CLAT/AILET/university law entrance → 5-yr integrated LLB (BA/BBA/BCom LLB) → Bar exam → practice or corporate firm
ExamsCLAT, AILET, state law entrances (e.g., MH CET Law), university tests
SkillsReading, logical reasoning, legal aptitude, debating, English

Corporate Counsel / Legal Advisor

In-house lawyer for companies, banks and startups
Active
LLB (5-yr or 3-yr after graduation)₹5–12 LPA
PathLLB (integrated after 12, or 3-yr after any graduation) → internships in firms/corporates → compliance & contracts roles
ExamsCLAT/state entrances for law; DU LLB etc. for 3-yr course
SkillsContract drafting, commercial awareness, negotiation, research

Judiciary / Public Law

Judge, public prosecutor, legal services authority roles
Long but stable
LLB + state judiciary exam₹8–12 LPA (judge post)
PathLLB → practice for the required years → state judicial services exam (or direct recruitment for some posts)
ExamsState Public Service Commission judicial service exams
SkillsProcedural law, judgement writing, integrity, patience
Sector 07

Design, media & creative careers

The most stream-agnostic sector. Portfolio beats marks here — start building work (sketches, videos, articles, small projects) from Class 11 itself.

Graphic / Visual Designer

Logos, packaging, social media and brand visuals
Portfolio-based
Any stream₹3–8 LPA
PathAny stream → B.Des / BFA / diplomas in design → strong portfolio → agency, in-house studio or freelance
ExamsNID DAT, UCEED, NIFT, college-level design tests
SkillsAdobe suite/Figma, typography, colour sense, client communication

Fashion Designer

Apparel design, textiles and fashion brands
NIFT / NID
Any stream₹3–8 LPA
PathClass 12 (any stream) → NIFT/NID/university design entrance → B.Des Fashion → internships → brands, labels or own line
ExamsNIFT entrance (GAT + CAT + situation test), NID DAT
SkillsSketching, sewing/textiles, trend awareness, branding

Animator / Video Editor / VFX Artist

Films, OTT, ads, games and YouTube content
Booming
Any stream₹3–9 LPA (freelance can exceed)
PathAny stream → B.Des/BFA/animation diploma or self-taught + short courses → studio, agency or freelance projects
ExamsDesign/animation college entrances (no national mandatory exam)
SkillsPremiere/AE/Blender/Maya, storytelling, timing, editing rhythm

Journalist / Content Creator

Reporting, editing and digital media
Active
Arts preferred, any stream OK₹3–8 LPA
PathClass 12 → BA Journalism/Mass Communication (CUET/university entrances) or any degree + writing portfolio → newsroom, digital publisher, own channel
ExamsCUET, IIMC/university mass-communication entrances
SkillsWriting, interviewing, fact-checking, video/audio editing

Hotel Management / Chef

Hotels, restaurants, culinary arts, cruise ships
Active
Any stream₹3–7 LPA
PathClass 12 (any stream) → NCHM JEE / IHM or private institute → B.Sc Hospitality or culinary course → hotels, cruise liners, own restaurant
ExamsNCHM JEE (NTA), state/university hotel-management entrances
SkillsEnglish, service attitude, stamina, teamwork, hygiene discipline

Cabin Crew / Aviation Services

Airline cabin crew, ground and airport services
Active
10+2, good English₹4–9 LPA
PathClass 12 → airline recruitment drives or cabin-crew training institutes → grooming + safety training → crew
ExamsAirline aptitude tests + interviews (no national exam)
SkillsCommunication, grooming, height/vision requirements, composure
Sector 08

Science & research careers

For students who want to discover, not just apply. ISRO, DRDO, CSIR labs, biotech and forensics need strong fundamentals from Class 11 onward.

Scientist / Researcher

ISRO, DRDO, CSIR labs, national research institutes
Nation-building
PCM / PCB₹7–15 LPA
PathPCM/PCB → B.Tech/B.Sc → M.Tech/M.Sc → GATE / CSIR-NET / ISRO-ICRB / DRDO recruitment → scientist post
ExamsJEE/CUET for UG, then GATE, CSIR-NET, institute-specific tests
SkillsDeep physics/maths/biology, research method, patience, publishing

Forensic Scientist

Crime labs, DNA, cyber forensics and ballistics
Rare, respected
PCB / PCM₹4–8 LPA
PathPCB/PCM → B.Sc Forensic Science → M.Sc → state/central forensic labs, police, agencies
ExamsUniversity entrances for B.Sc/M.Sc Forensic Science; govt lab recruitment
SkillsChemistry/biology lab work, observation, chain-of-custody discipline

Food Technologist

Food safety, processing and product development
Growing
PCB / PCM₹3.5–8 LPA
PathPCB/PCM → B.Tech/B.Sc Food Technology → FMCG companies, quality labs, QA/QC or R&D
ExamsICAR/CUET/state entrances for food technology programmes
SkillsChemistry & microbiology, process discipline, quality standards

Environmental Scientist

Climate, pollution control, sustainability and EIA
Future-proof
Science background₹3–7 LPA
PathAny science stream → B.Sc Environmental Science → M.Sc → pollution control boards, consultancies, CSR/sustainability teams
ExamsCUET and university entrances; later UGC NET for research/teaching
SkillsField sampling, GIS/data tools, report writing, policy awareness

Biotechnologist

Genetics, vaccines, pharma R&D and agri-biotech
Research-heavy
PCB₹3.5–8 LPA
PathPCB → B.Sc/B.Tech Biotechnology → M.Sc → pharma/biotech companies or research institutes
ExamsCUET, university entrances, GAT-B for PG
SkillsLab technique, molecular biology, data analysis, persistence

Pilot

Commercial aviation — airline or private
Expensive training
PCM (for many routes)₹10–25 LPA+
PathPCM in Class 12 → DGCA-approved flying school → CPL (Commercial Pilot Licence) with medicals → type rating → airline; or Air Force via NDA
ExamsDGCA exams + flying hours; medical fitness is the big filter
SkillsPhysics/maths, situational awareness, English, health & eyesight
Sector 09

Agriculture & environment careers

Often overlooked and quietly one of the most government-employable sectors in India — plus agri-tech startups are booming.

Agriculture Officer / B.Sc Agriculture

Government agriculture services, research and agri-business
Govt sector opens
PCB / PCM / Agri stream₹3.5–8 LPA
PathClass 12 (PCB/PCM/Agriculture) → B.Sc Agriculture (4 yrs) via ICAR/CUET/state entrances → state agriculture services, banks (agri officer), agri-companies
ExamsICAR/CUET/state agricultural university entrances; later IBPS AFO and state exams
SkillsAgronomy, soil science, extension communication, field work stamina

Agri-Entrepreneur / Modern Farming

Own farm business, dairy, poultry, agri-tech services
Active
Any streamVariable — scalable
PathAny stream → B.Sc Agri / agri-diploma / family experience + government schemes (soil health, drip irrigation, KCC loans) → own agri-enterprise
ExamsNo entrance exam; state agriculture department schemes assist entry
SkillsBusiness sense, mechanics of farming, market linkage, weather/nutrition knowledge

Agri-Tech / Food Processing Specialist

Modernise the food supply chain with data and machinery
Modern
PCB / PCM₹4–9 LPA
PathPCB/PCM → B.Tech Food Tech / Agri Engineering / B.Sc Agri → startups, FMCG supply chains, agri-input companies
ExamsICAR/CUET, state entrances, JEE for agri engineering
SkillsSupply chains, IoT basics, data analysis, field trials
Sector 10

Education & social impact careers

For students who want their work to directly help people. Works from any stream — and pairs beautifully with any subject interest.

Social Worker / NGO Professional

Community development, child rights, women's empowerment, disaster relief
Meaningful
Any stream₹2.5–6 LPA
PathAny graduation (BA Social Work preferred) → MSW → NGOs, government programs, CSR teams, international agencies
ExamsCUET/university entrances for BA & MSW
SkillsEmpathy, field work, communication, project management, local languages

Public Policy Analyst

Design and evaluate government policies and programs
Elite niche
Arts / Commerce₹4–10 LPA
PathBA Economics/Pol Science → MA Public Policy (e.g., at leading institutions) → think tanks, government advisory, consulting
ExamsCUET and university entrances (UG & PG levels)
SkillsEconomics, statistics, writing, reading policy documents, data

EdTech / Online Educator

Teach online, create courses, tutor students across India
Skill-based
Any stream (strong subject needed)₹3–10 LPA
PathAny graduation + mastery of one subject → teach on platforms, create content, tutor; grow your own channel
ExamsNone mandatory; teaching certifications help credibility
SkillsClear explanation, camera comfort, planning, student empathy
Sector 11

Skilled trades & technical careers (ITI)

India's skilled labour shortage is real. ITI trades give you an employable skill in under two years, with apprenticeships that pay a stipend while you learn.

Electrician

Wiring, installation, maintenance — everywhere from homes to factories
High demand
ITI Electrician₹1.8–5 LPA
PathClass 10 → ITI Electrician (2 yrs) → apprenticeship → industrial/construction jobs, government electrical posts, or own electrical business
ExamsState ITI admission; then All India Trade Test for certification
SkillsSafety, circuits, tools, customer handling, problem diagnosis

Fitter / Machinist / Welder

Precision manufacturing, fabrication and maintenance trades
Industry backbone
ITI trade₹2–5 LPA
PathClass 10 → ITI trade course (1–2 yrs) → apprenticeship in PSU/factory → technician roles, railway/defence/industrial jobs, or fabrication business
ExamsState ITI admission; NCVT/SCVT trade certificate
SkillsBlueprint reading, precision, tool handling, safety, physical stamina

COPA / Computer Operator

Office computing, data entry, hardware basics and support
Easy entry
ITI COPA₹1.8–4.5 LPA
PathClass 10 → ITI COPA (1 yr) → data entry/office jobs, IT support, further certifications (Tally, Excel, networking)
ExamsState ITI admission; trade test
SkillsTyping speed, MS Office, basic networking, accuracy

Mechanic (Motor Vehicle / AC & Refrigeration)

Automobile and cooling system service technician
Self-employment friendly
ITI trade₹2–5 LPA
PathClass 10 → ITI Mechanic (1–2 yrs) → service centres, EV workshops (fast-growing), or own garage
ExamsState ITI admission; trade certification
SkillsEngine/diagnostics knowledge, electricals, customer service, EV basics
Sector 12

Hospitality, travel & transport careers

Travelling work, global mobility and quick entry after 10+2. Hotels, cruise lines, shipping and tourism reward service attitude over marks.

Hotel & Hospitality Manager

Front office, F&B, events and resorts
Active
10+2 any stream₹3–7 LPA
PathClass 12 → NCHM JEE / IHM B.Sc → management trainee → department head; international hotel chains recruit actively
ExamsNCHM JEE, state hotel-management entrances
SkillsEnglish & another language, guest service, operations, leadership

Merchant Navy Officer

Sailing officer on cargo and cruise ships
High pay, sea life
PCM for officer route₹6–20 LPA
PathPCM → B.Sc Nautical Science / B.Tech Marine Engineering (CETs like IMU-CET) → cadetship → officer ranks. GP rating/crew courses have their own eligibility — verify each
ExamsIMU CET and institute tests; medical fitness mandatory
SkillsPhysics & maths, discipline, English, life at sea resilience

Travel & Tourism Professional

Tour operations, travel tech, guiding and destination management
Recovering fast
Any stream₹2.5–6 LPA
PathClass 12 → BA/BBA Tourism or diploma → travel agencies, OTAs, airlines, own tour business
ExamsUniversity entrances / CUET for tourism programmes
SkillsLanguages, geography, booking software, storytelling, sales

Logistics & Warehouse Operations

The supply chain that moves everything in India
Booming
10th/ITI/12th₹2.5–6 LPA
PathClass 10/12 → warehouse/3PL jobs or logistics diplomas → supervisor → operations manager; e-commerce warehouses hire at scale
ExamsNo national exam; certifications (SCM basics) help growth
SkillsInventory systems, WMS software, forklift/licence, team supervision
