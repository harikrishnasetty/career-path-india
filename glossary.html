Every confusing word, explained in one line

Short forms, exam names and career jargon — PCM, NEET, lateral entry, LPA and more — written the way you would explain them to a younger cousin. Type a word, or tap a letter.

AYUSH AYUSH

Traditional Indian medicine systems — Ayurveda, Yoga, Unani, Siddha and Homeopathy. You can do BAMS and similar degrees through NEET UG.

Allied health sciences AHS

Health careers that support doctors: physiotherapy, radiology, lab technology, optometry, nutrition, OT technician and more. Often entered after PCB via NEET or state exams.

B.Arch Bachelor of Architecture

A 5-year degree to become an architect (someone who designs buildings). Admission is usually via NATA or JEE Main Paper 2; PCM in Class 11–12 is required.

BBA / BCA / B.Des / B.Com

Popular 3-year degrees after Class 12: BBA = business administration, BCA = computer applications (software without needing PCM), B.Des = design, B.Com = commerce. Many admit through CUET or university tests.

BITSAT

The entrance exam for BITS Pilani and its campuses. It is a separate exam from JEE, taken by Class 12 PCM students.

Board exam

The Class 10 and Class 12 exams set by your education board (CBSE, ICSE or a state board). Your marks here decide stream and college options.

CA Chartered Accountant

A respected finance professional who audits accounts and advises on tax. Route: after Class 12 (any stream, Commerce helps) clear CA Foundation → Intermediate → Final.

CBSE / ICSE / State board

The organisations that design your school syllabus and conduct board exams. CBSE is national, ICSE is national with a different pattern, and each state has its own board.

CET Common Entrance Test

A state-level entrance exam for engineering, medical or other courses, e.g. MHT-CET, KCET, WBJEE. It is an alternative or addition to national exams.

CLAT Common Law Admission Test

The main entrance for 5-year law degrees at National Law Universities. Open to students from any stream after Class 12.

CMA / CS

CMA = Cost and Management Accountant; CS = Company Secretary. Both are professional finance/law-adjacent courses you can start after Class 12 (CS via CSEET).

CUET UG Common University Entrance Test

A single national test for admission to most central and many other universities (like Delhi University) for undergraduate courses. You choose subjects; it is open to all streams.

Diploma (Polytechnic)

A 3-year practical engineering/technical course you can join right after Class 10. It leads to junior engineer jobs and lateral entry into B.Tech later.

Domicile

Proof that you are a resident of a particular state. Many state exams, colleges and scholarships reserve seats or give preference to domicile students.

EWS / OBC / SC / ST

Categories used for reservation (quota) in colleges and government jobs. EWS = economically weaker section; OBC = other backward classes; SC/ST = scheduled castes/tribes.

Entrance exam

Any competitive test used for admission — JEE, NEET, CUET, CLAT, NDA and so on. Your marks in this exam, not your board marks, often decide which college you get.

GATE

Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering — taken after a bachelor's degree for M.Tech, research or PSU jobs. Not for school students.

Hostel / cutoff

Hostel = student accommodation at a college. Cutoff = the minimum marks/rank needed for admission in a given round; it changes each year.

ICAR

Indian Council of Agricultural Research. It runs the national entrance (CUET-based in recent years) for agriculture degrees like B.Sc Agriculture.

IIT / NIT / IIIT

India's top engineering institutes: IITs (Indian Institutes of Technology), NITs (National Institutes of Technology) and IIITs. IITs admit via JEE Advanced; NITs and IIITs mainly via JEE Main counselling (JoSAA).

INSPIRE-SHE

A government scholarship (about ₹80,000 per year) given to bright science students. It replaced the old KVPY scheme, which ended in 2022.

Integrated course

A combined programme, e.g. a 5-year integrated B.Tech+M.Tech or BA+LLB, where two stages are done together instead of separately.

ITI Industrial Training Institute

Vocational institutes offering short trade courses (electrician, fitter, welder, COPA and more) after Class 10. You get an NCVT/SCVT certificate and can start working quickly.

JEE Main / JEE Advanced

JEE Main is the national engineering entrance (also used for NITs, IIITs, and as a JEE Advanced qualifier). JEE Advanced is the tougher exam for IIT admission, taken only by top JEE Main scorers.

JoSAA

The online counselling system that allots engineering seats in IITs, NITs, IIITs and GFTIs based on JEE ranks.

KVPY

Kishore Vaigyanik Protsahan Yojana — a science scholarship that was discontinued in 2022. Its benefits moved into INSPIRE-SHE.

Lateral entry

Joining a course partway through. Common example: a Diploma holder joining B.Tech directly in the second year instead of the first.

LOV / Seat allotment

In counselling, seat allotment is the process where the system gives you a college seat based on your rank and choices. (LOV = List of Values, a dropdown menu in software.)

LPA Lakhs Per Annum

How salary is often quoted in India. ₹6 LPA = 6,00,000 rupees per year (about ₹50,000 per month before tax).

MBBS / BDS

MBBS = the degree to become a doctor; BDS = to become a dentist. Both are entered through NEET UG after Class 12 with PCB.

Merit list

A ranked list of students by marks or exam score, used to decide admission. Being higher on the merit list means a better chance of your preferred college/stream.

NCERT

The body that writes the standard school textbooks used across India. Its Class 11–12 books are the base for most entrance exams.

NEET UG National Eligibility cum Entrance Test

The single national entrance for MBBS, BDS, AYUSH and many allied health courses. Taken after Class 12 with Biology (PCB).

NEP 2020

National Education Policy 2020 — the reform that, among other things, introduced multiple board attempts and a more flexible, multi-disciplinary school and college system.

NIOS

National Institute of Open Schooling. It lets you complete Class 10/12 through open learning, change subjects, or study alongside other work — useful if you want a second chance or flexibility.

NIRF ranking

The government's official ranking of Indian colleges and universities by category. A helpful (not perfect) way to compare institutes.

NSP National Scholarship Portal

The central website where you apply for most government scholarships. One place for many schemes — check eligibility carefully.

NTA

National Testing Agency — the government body that conducts JEE Main, NEET UG, CUET UG, NCHM JEE and several other national exams.

Olympiad

Competitive knowledge exams beyond school (e.g. HBCSE science Olympiads, maths Olympiads). Winning can open doors to camps, scholarships and international contests.

PCB Physics, Chemistry, Biology

The Science stream combination for medical, biology and health careers. Required for NEET UG.

PCM Physics, Chemistry, Maths

The Science stream combination for engineering, architecture, technical defence and data careers. Required for most B.Tech and B.Arch admissions.

PCMB

Physics, Chemistry, Maths and Biology together — keeps both engineering (JEE) and medical (NEET) options open, but the workload is heavier.

Polytechnic

An institute offering 3-year diploma courses in engineering and technology after Class 10. Also called a "diploma" route.

PSU

Public Sector Undertaking — a government-owned company like ONGC, BHEL or Indian Railways. PSU jobs are usually via exams after graduation.

Reservation / Quota

Seats set aside for specific groups (SC, ST, OBC, EWS, PwD, domicile, etc.) in colleges and jobs. Rules differ between states and institutions.

Seat matrix

The total number of seats available in each course and category at a college — useful to judge how competitive an admission will be.

SGPA / CGPA

Grading in college: SGPA = your score in one semester, CGPA = the running average across all semesters so far.

SSC / UPSC

Two big government recruitment bodies. SSC (Staff Selection Commission) conducts exams for many central government posts; UPSC conducts the prestigious Civil Services (IAS/IPS) exam after graduation.

Stream

The subject group you choose in Class 11–12: Science, Commerce or Arts — or a vocational/technical route like Diploma or ITI.

UCEED

Undergraduate Common Entrance Examination for Design (by IIT Bombay). For admission to B.Des programmes at IITs and other design schools. Open to any stream.

Vocational course

Skill-based training (e.g. in agriculture, hospitality, media, IT support) designed to make you job-ready quickly, often with government backing.
