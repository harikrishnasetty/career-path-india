Good to know

Before you trust any quiz (including this one)

✓

Interests beat marks

Marks decide which stream you can get; interests decide whether you'll enjoy it for years. This quiz measures interests only. If your marks block a route, look at the nearest alternative (e.g. BCA instead of B.Tech).

!

Nobody should decide for you

Peer pressure, family expectations and "log kya kahenge" are the biggest reasons students get stuck. Use the result to start a conversation, not to end one.

i

Skills can be learned

You do not need to be "born talented" at coding, drawing or accounting. Interest plus consistent practice is what actually builds a career.

✗

Streams are not cages

Commerce students become lawyers, arts students become entrepreneurs and science students join civil services every year. Stream is a starting lane, not a life sentence.
