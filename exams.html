Also in school: things that are not "exams"

Class 9 and 10 are for building fundamentals: board-pattern tests, school-level aptitude counseling, career fairs, free online courses (Diksha, SWAYAM, Khan Academy), and sports/NCC/reading habits. No exam in Class 9 or 10 decides your career. Use this time to explore streams (see the roadmap).

Board exam reform

CBSE Class 10 board exams are now held twice a year (from 2026)

This is the biggest change in Indian school exams in a decade, introduced under NEP 2020. If you are in Class 9 or 10 today, this is your system.

How the two-exam system works

  • Exam 1 — February: compulsory for every Class 10 student, full syllabus, internal assessments already done once before this exam
  • Exam 2 — May: optional improvement attempt; you can improve in up to three subjects (Science, Mathematics, Social Science and languages)
  • Results: first phase in April, second phase in June; the better score of the two is retained for each subject
  • Fee: collected for both exams at the time of application
  • Both editions run on the full syllabus; the second edition also acts as the supplementary exam — no separate special exams are conducted

What it means for you

  • One bad day in February no longer ruins your Class 10 report card — you get a genuine second chance in May
  • Winter-bound schools, sportspersons and students with special needs get flexible session choices
  • Stress and coaching dependence should drop, because "high stakes" is reduced
  • Start improving from the first attempt itself — do not treat February as a practice test
  • Class 12 may get a similar two-exam plan later; CBSE has indicated it is being considered (not yet notified)

State boards (Bihar, UP, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, CBSE-adjacent state boards etc.) run their own schedules — check your board's official site.

✗

KVPY no longer exists — do not wait for it

Kishore Vaigyanik Protsahan Yojana (KVPY) was permanently discontinued in 2022 by the Department of Science and Technology and merged into INSPIRE. Old KVPY fellows continue receiving their fellowship, but no new KVPY exam or application will ever be conducted. If a coaching institute promises "KVPY preparation", it is outdated.

Exam system changes

What is changing in the exam system (2025–26)

Rules for big exams are in motion. You will be writing these exams in 2027–2029, so keep an eye on them.

NEET UG: 2026 cancellation & proposed reforms

NEET (UG) 2026 was held on 3 May, cancelled on 12 May over question-match concerns, and re-conducted on 21 June; results followed on 16 July. A Parliamentary committee has recommended (not yet notified): an upper age limit and attempt cap, a two-stage format (common prelim + subject mains), and a gradual shift to computer-based testing.

Action: follow only nta.ac.in and neet.nta.nic.in — rumours spread fast during exam seasons.

CUET UG: format under review

CUET UG 2026 saw 15.7 lakh candidates across 321 Indian cities plus 15 international centres, with an average of 4.3 subjects each. Proposals under review include shorter duration, fewer subject choices and a new grouping so students finish in a single day.

Action: even if the format changes, your Class 11 subjects must cover the CUET domain subjects you will need.

NTSE: revival push

The Parliamentary Standing Committee on Education reported in August 2026 that NTSE's suspension since 2021 is a "severe blow to the talent pipeline" and urged the ministry to explore reviving it.

Action: if you are in Class 10, watch ncert.nic.in around the usual August–October notification window — but do not block your plans on it.
