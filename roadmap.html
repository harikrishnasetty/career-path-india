The Class 9 → career roadmap

One clear timeline from the year you are in right now to your first real job. Read the step that matches your current class, then look at the month-by-month exam calendar so nothing takes you by surprise.

Step by step

Seven stages, in order

You do not have to plan everything today. You only have to know the next step.

Now — Class 9

Explore everything, decide nothing

  • Focus on basics: Maths, Science, English and Social Science — every stream needs them.
  • Notice which subjects you genuinely enjoy, not just where you score.
  • Try one or two extra things: coding, drawing, a sport, debating, a school olympiad.
  • Look at careers around you and ask adults what their day at work is really like.
Class 10 (board year)

Score well, but keep options open

  • Give the board exam your full attention — your Class 10 marks decide which stream and which school you can get.
  • From 2026, CBSE runs the Class 10 board exam twice a year: the February exam is compulsory, the May exam is an optional improvement attempt in up to three subjects. The better score is kept.
  • Do the Stream Finder quiz and read the streams page while exams are on, so you are not rushing in May.
  • Keep an eye on scholarship tests you may be eligible for (see the calendar below).
Just after 10th — the big decision

Choose your route for Class 11

  • Pick from: Science (PCM / PCB / PCMB), Commerce, Arts/Humanities, Polytechnic Diploma (3 years, after 10th), ITI trade, or a vocational/agriculture course.
  • Class 11 admission is mostly on Class 10 marks — check your state's rules and each school's cut-off.
  • Diploma and ITI students start a job-ready course immediately instead of Class 11 — you can still do a degree later.
  • Don't choose a stream only because a friend did. Read what each one actually leads to first.
Class 11

Set the foundation — and begin planning your goal exam

  • This is the year the real syllabus begins. Build strong basics in your chosen subjects.
  • If your goal is engineering, medicine, law, design or a top university, this is when you start light, steady preparation for JEE / NEET / CLAT / NIFT / CUET — not a crash course at the last minute.
  • Start one hobby or skill project you can talk about later (a small app, a blog, a portfolio, a sports achievement).
  • Learn to use the official portals: NTA, your board, and the National Scholarship Portal.
Class 12

Board exams + entrance exams, back to back

  • Boards usually run February–April (board and state dates differ — confirm on your board's site).
  • Major entrances fall between January and June: JEE Main (January + April), NEET UG (May), CUET UG (May), plus NDA, CLAT, NIFT, NID, UCEED, NATA and state tests.
  • Applications for most of these open between November (Class 12) and March — missing a form is the most common, most painful mistake.
  • Keep a simple calendar and note every last date the moment a notification comes out.
Graduation (3–5 years)

Degree, skills and experience together

  • Do not just attend classes. Internships, projects, competitions and certifications are what get you hired.
  • Keep preparing for higher goals: GATE, CAT, UPSC, CA/CS/CMA inter levels, or a master's.
  • Build a network — seniors, alumni, teachers and online communities in your field.
  • Learn one practical tool deeply (a language, a design tool, Excel, lab technique — whatever fits your field).
First job & beyond

Start, then keep growing

  • Your first job is a start, not a destination. Learn from seniors and take feedback seriously.
  • Government jobs (SSC, banking, state services, defence) are open to most streams — keep that door in mind too.
  • Re-skill every year; the job you do at 30 may not exist today, and the one that will may not exist yet.

This calendar is a compass, not a timetable

NTA, CBSE, UPSC and state boards revise dates, add sessions and change eligibility almost every year. Always open the official website for the exact notification before you plan anything around a date you read here.

Pick your year

What to do this year, whichever class you are in

In Class 9

Build basics, explore hobbies and interests, and read the streams page once just to understand what exists. No pressure to decide.

In Class 10

Score as well as you can, do the Stream Finder quiz, talk to your parents and teachers, and shortlist two or three streams that genuinely interest you.

Just after 10th

Fill your Class 11 admission (or Diploma/ITI) form carefully, note every deadline, and start a light study habit for your goal subjects.

In Class 11

Pick your goal (engineering / medicine / law / design / civil services / other), begin steady prep, and start one skill project. Register on the National Scholarship Portal if eligible.

In Class 12

Track every entrance application date without fail. Balance board and entrance revision. Keep your documents ready well before counselling begins.
